# SUPER PROMPT: BUILD A UNIVERSAL SUPERMARKET SCHEME ENGINE

You are a senior retail ERP/POS architect and full-stack developer.

I am building a supermarket/FMCG retail management system. Create a **production-ready Universal Scheme Management & Scheme Calculation Engine** that can support simple as well as advanced supermarket promotional schemes.

The system must be designed so that new scheme types can be added later without rewriting the billing engine.

---

# 1. OBJECTIVE

Build a centralized **Scheme Engine** that:

* Creates and manages promotional schemes
* Automatically identifies eligible schemes during billing
* Calculates the best applicable benefit
* Supports product, category, brand, customer, store and bill-level schemes
* Handles quantity and value slabs
* Supports Buy X Get Y
* Supports Combo and Mix & Match
* Supports free gifts
* Supports coupons/vouchers
* Supports time/date-based schemes
* Supports supplier-funded schemes
* Supports loyalty-point eligibility
* Prevents incorrect stacking of schemes
* Shows scheme details clearly on POS bills
* Maintains complete audit history
* Provides scheme performance reports

Do NOT hard-code promotional logic inside POS billing.

Create a separate reusable:

`SCHEME ENGINE`

that can be called by:

* POS Billing
* E-commerce
* Mobile App
* Customer Portal
* Order Management
* Reports
* Loyalty System

Do not assume every scheme discount has identical GST treatment.
